On the call

What we will cover

The project, how the EB-5 process runs, what the timing looks like from where you stand, and what it would take to file before the 31 December deadline. If EB-5 is not a sensible route for you, we will say so on the call rather than put you into a follow-up sequence.

What to include in your email

Nothing is required, but the call is more useful if you can tell us your current immigration status, your country of birth, the timeframe in which you could file, and whether you are already working with an immigration attorney. Country of birth matters more than people expect — it determines which visa queue applies and how long it is likely to run.

Who we can speak with

The offering is open to verified accredited investors only. Verification happens later in the process, through documentation rather than self-certification, and never from an email alone.
